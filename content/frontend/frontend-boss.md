## story | Before the redesign ships

A designer handed over a new signup page built quickly in plain HTML, outside the design system. Rumana: "Review it the way a screen reader, a keyboard and a tired user on a sunny street would experience it. Then we'll rebuild it with our components."

## model | A quick accessibility review

1. Images: meaningful ones have descriptive `alt`, decorative ones `alt=""`.
2. Forms: every input has a label.
3. Interactive things are real `<button>`s and `<a>`s.
4. Headings are real headings, in order.
5. Text contrast meets 4.5:1.
